## 1. Executive Summary

RehabYangu is a **multi-tenant, white-label SaaS platform** designed specifically for rehabilitation centres, psychiatric hospitals, addiction treatment facilities, and mental health organisations across Africa.

The platform is a complete digital operating system for behavioural healthcare – managing patients, clinical documentation, pharmacy, billing, sponsors, inventory, reporting, and compliance.

**The Problem:**
- Rehabilitation centres in Kenya and East Africa rely on paper-based records or fragmented systems.
- Staff waste hours on manual documentation, billing errors are common, and sponsors struggle to track payments.
- No unified, affordable, and scalable platform exists for this sector.

**The Solution:**
- RehabYangu digitises and streamlines every aspect of facility operations.
- It is secure, compliant, and designed to scale from a single facility to hundreds.
- Tenants can white‑label the platform, making it feel like their own system.

**The Opportunity:**
- Kenya has 50+ registered rehabilitation centres, with more opening each year.
- Across East Africa, the number exceeds 200+ facilities.
- Demand is growing due to increasing mental health awareness and government focus on addiction treatment.

**Our Goal:**
- Launch with Serenity Place Treatment Center (Kenya) as the anchor customer.
- Scale to 20+ facilities by Year 2, and 100+ by Year 5.
- Become the most trusted behavioural healthcare platform in Africa.

## 5. Competitive Landscape

### 5.1 Competitors

| Competitor | Type | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|------|-----------|------------|
| **OpenMRS** | Open-source EMR | Free, widely used | Complex, not specialised for rehab |

| **Manual Systems** (Excel/Paper) | Informal | Low cost | Inefficient, error-prone |

### 5.2 RehabYangu's Differentiators

| Differentiator | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Specialisation** | Built specifically for rehab and mental health |
| **White‑labelling** | Each facility feels like it's their own system |
| **Multi‑tenant SaaS** | Scalable from 1 to 100+ facilities |
| **Sponsor Management** | Built-in invoicing and payment tracking for sponsors |
| **Affordable Pricing** | Designed for the East African market |
| **Local Support** | In‑country implementation and training |

## 11. Key Risks & Mitigations

| **Regulatory changes** | Low | High | Flexible architecture; legal advisor |
